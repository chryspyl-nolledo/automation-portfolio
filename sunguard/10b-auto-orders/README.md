# 10B — Auto orders and inventory/order confirmation

Two workflows exported as safe imports:
- `10B_SunGuard_Auto_Orders.json` — picks up new orders automatically.
- `10B_SunGuard_Inventory_Confirmation.json` — checks stock and confirms the order (dry-run style, no real customer e-mails).

Replace `YOUR_GOOGLE_SHEET_ID` and connect your own Google credentials.
